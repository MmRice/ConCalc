A unit key pressed with a result on the display tags that value instead of clearing it.

Conv + Feet switches between decimal feet and feet-inches.

Conv + Inch switches between decimal and fractional inches.

From yards or metric, the first Conv + Feet shows feet-and-inches and the second shows decimal feet. The first Conv + Inch shows fractional inches and the second shows decimal inches.

Conv is also the second-function key.

Press Conv and the keys swap their labels to their second functions.

A small dot marks the keys that have one.

The CONV indicator lights up in the sub row while it is on.

Press Conv again, or use the second function, and the labels go back.

Basic math

You see: 120 ft²

Press: 120 Feet Feet ÷ 10 Feet =

You see: 12′

Press: 5 + 3 =

You see: 8

Press: 43 + 16 = = =

You see: 59, then 75, then 91 — pressing = again repeats the last step, which is how you walk out even spacings. Typing a number or pressing another key stops the repeat — except M+ and Rcl, which leave it armed. Clearing stops it too.

+ and − need the same kind of value on both sides. Adding a length to an area does nothing — the display keeps the second number, and the first number and the + stay waiting.

Keys that stay lit

The key you pressed stays black until the job is done.

Press: 7 ×

You see: × stays black while you enter the second number, until you press =.

Press: Diag with Rise and Run stored

You see: Diag stays black while its answer is on the display. Typing a new number, or pressing another key, clears it — except inside a solver, where the solver key stays black while it asks for numbers or steps through answers.

A store press lights its number key the same way: Conv + 7 lights 7 until another key clears it.

While an operation waits for its second number, the operator key is the one that stays black.

History and clear

Tap the display to open History.

Tap the display again to close History.

Tap a row to load its answer back into the calculator.

Use the up and down arrows to page through it, 8 entries per page, newest first.

Drag the bar on the right, or tap it, to jump through your history.

Swipe up or down on the History list to move a page at a time — swipe up goes to the next page.

The Guide tab has the same bar on the right, and you can swipe up and down on the guide to move a page at a time.

The last 100 entries are kept. The oldest drops off on its own.

Tap the Guide tab at the top to open this guide.

Tap the History tab to go back to History.

Tap ⌫ / C to delete one character. On a result with nothing to delete, a tap does nothing.

Hold ⌫ / C about half a second to clear the display. The first hold clears what you typed, any operation waiting, the lit key, and the repeat — and puts the display back to feet-and-inches. It keeps the stored triangle, the stored settings, memory, Prefs, and History, and solver stepping carries on where it left off.

Hold it again to clear Rise, Run, Diagonal, and the irregular pitch too, and to restart solver stepping. Pitch stays, and the stored settings, memory, Prefs, and History stay too.

Press: Conv ×

You see: Clear All — everything above goes, plus Pitch, memory, and the stored settings: riser, tread, on-centre spacing, and weight-per-volume go back to their defaults. Prefs and History survive. Conv + × works from Prefs too.

Holding ⌫ / C while Prefs is open leaves Prefs and brings back the value you had. Holding it in a solver or a cost prompt leaves that prompt and shows 0″.

Holding it while Conv or Rcl is armed only disarms it — the display stays as it was.
